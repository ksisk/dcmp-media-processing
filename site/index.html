GPU policy

Each job type has a weight from 0 to 10 per card: when a job starts, the broker runs it on the free card with the highest weight (ties go to the card with fewer jobs, then the quieter one). 0 means never on that card. A card counts as free when it has at least the job type's VRAM floor available. Schedules change a weight inside a time window (Pacific time; a window may cross midnight) — e.g. Forager on the RTX 4080 at weight 0 by day and 8 from 19:00 to 07:00. Weights are preferences, not reservations: if the preferred card is busy, the job takes the best free one rather than waiting. Default weight applies to any card the row has no entry for, so a newly installed GPU is used straight away.
